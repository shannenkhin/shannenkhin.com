{%- comment -%} Renders a CV date range and its inclusive duration from `start`/`end`, each "YYYY-MM". An empty or absent `end` is an ongoing role, rendered as "… – Now" with no duration, because a duration that grows every month would be baked into the HTML at build time and be wrong by the following one. This replaces a hand-typed `date_range:` string in every collection file, which is why the separator used to be a hyphen in six of them and an en dash in the seventh, and why the current role was the only one without a duration. The durations the old strings carried are reproduced exactly by the inclusive count below — a role running Jan 2022 to May 2024 is 29 months, "2yrs 5mos" — so this changes no date on the page, only where they come from. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign sparts = include.start | split: "-" -%} {%- assign sy = sparts[0] -%} {%- assign sm = sparts[1] -%} {%- capture sd -%}{{ sy }}-{{ sm }}-01{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign start_label = sd | date: "%b %Y" -%} {%- if include.end and include.end != "" -%} {%- assign eparts = include.end | split: "-" -%} {%- assign ey = eparts[0] -%} {%- assign em = eparts[1] -%} {%- capture ed -%}{{ ey }}-{{ em }}-01{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign end_label = ed | date: "%b %Y" -%} {%- assign smon = sy | times: 12 | plus: sm -%} {%- assign emon = ey | times: 12 | plus: em -%} {%- assign total = emon | minus: smon | plus: 1 -%} {%- assign years = total | divided_by: 12 -%} {%- assign months = total | modulo: 12 -%} {%- capture ys -%}{%- if years > 0 -%}{{ years }}yr{%- unless years == 1 -%}s{%- endunless -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endcapture -%} {%- capture ms -%}{%- if months > 0 -%}{{ months }}mo{%- unless months == 1 -%}s{%- endunless -%}{%- endif -%}{%- endcapture -%} {%- if ys != "" and ms != "" -%}{%- assign dur = ys | append: " " | append: ms -%}{%- else -%}{%- assign dur = ys | append: ms -%}{%- endif -%} {{- start_label }} – {{ end_label }} ({{ dur }}) {%- else -%} {{- start_label }} – Now {%- endif -%}
